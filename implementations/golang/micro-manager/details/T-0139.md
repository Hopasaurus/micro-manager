# T-0139 — Conditional polling (D2): poll only while the SSE stream is down

Report option D2 of `research-app-fllicker.md` §3 D, promoted to an item by the
T-0131 measurement, which found it to be a better next step than the whole of
Phase 2.

## Why this and not Phase 2

The T-0131 numbers (§8 of the report) say the idle refresh traffic is:

- **100% backstop, 0% events.** Over 33 minutes on an idle project the tab made
  65 status fetches and observed **zero** SSE events. The `every 30s` clause is
  the entire idle load.
- **100% waste.** 20/20 refetches of board, status and check were byte-identical
  on a quiescent directory — proven, not sampled, because the fragment is a pure
  function of the files.

Phase 2 (T-0132–T-0134) makes event-driven refreshes fewer and smaller. On an
idle project there are no event-driven refreshes to shrink. D2 removes the
traffic that actually exists.

## Requirements

- In `mm.js`, stop relying on an unconditional `every 30s`:
  - on `htmx:sseOpen`, no polling;
  - on `htmx:sseClose` / `htmx:sseError`, start a ~5 s interval that issues the
    same fragment fetches through `htmx.ajax()`;
  - on reconnect, stop it again.
- `spec-gui.md` §2.3 requires the view to converge with **polling alone**, and
  `architecture.md` §4.5 already specifies that an SSE-disabled configuration
  renders plain poll triggers. Keep that path: D2 changes the behaviour when
  SSE is *available*, not the guarantee when it is not.
- `sse_test.go` asserts the `every 30s` trigger strings. Those assertions change
  deliberately here — rewrite them to assert the new wiring rather than deleting
  them.
- Do not start until [[T-0138]] is resolved: that regression leaks a poll chain
  per mutation, and measuring or changing polling on top of it would be
  measuring the leak.

## Open questions

- Whether the check view keeps a backstop regardless — it is a read-only view
  where a stale answer is cheap, and revalidating every 30 s for nothing is the
  single most wasteful poll in the app.
- Whether the interval should back off rather than stay at 5 s.
